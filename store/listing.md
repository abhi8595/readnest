## Full description
ReadNest is a cozy home for your ebooks. Import EPUBs, PDFs, comics and
documents, and keep them organized by author, series and collections.
No sign-up needed — your library stays on your device.

READER THAT FEELS LIKE PAPER
- Warm Day, Sepia, Night, Console and OLED themes
- Beautiful serif type with adjustable size, spacing and margins
- Swipe pages or continuous scroll, with hyphenation
- Bookmarks, highlights, notes and a built-in dictionary

READ-ALOUD
- Natural text-to-speech with sleep timer
- Premium: background listening with the screen locked

EVERYTHING IN ONE PLACE
- Quotes & Notes hub: every highlight across every book, searchable
- Shelves for series, genres and reading lists
- Duplicate detection by content fingerprint, not just filename
- Reading stats and streaks

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- Local-first: no account, no tracking SDKs
- Optional Google Drive sync (Premium) backs up books, progress and notes
- Free tier uses non-personalized ads only

FORMATS
EPUB · PDF · MOBI · AZW3 · CBZ · FB2 · TXT · DOCX · ODT

ReadNest Premium (optional): ad-free reading, Drive sync, background
read-aloud, custom fonts, extra highlight colors and page thumbnails.
