## Long description (keyword-seeded, natural)

**ReadNest is a cozy home for all your ebooks.** This fast ebook reader opens EPUB,
PDF, MOBI, AZW3, TXT, FB2, DOCX, ODT and comic formats CBZ/CBR — then organizes
everything into a clean offline book library. No account. No sign-up. Just reading.

**YOUR LIBRARY, ORGANIZED**
• Auto-scan folders for books & documents (EPUB reader + PDF reader + document reader)
• Browse by author, series, format, or your own collections
• Sort by title, author, size, or recent reads
• Duplicate detection keeps your shelves tidy
• Folders view shows what's where on your device

**A READER THAT FEELS LIKE PAPER**
• Warm Day, Sepia, Night, Console and OLED dark mode reader themes
• Beautiful serif fonts, adjustable size, boldness, spacing and margins
• Page or scroll mode, hyphenation, brightness and orientation controls
• Comic reader with fast paging for CBZ/CBR

**READ ALOUD (TEXT TO SPEECH)**
• Natural text to speech reader with voice and speed controls
• Sentence highlighting follows along as it reads
• Sleep timer for bedtime chapters

**NOTES, QUOTES & DICTIONARY**
• Bookmarks, highlights in multiple colors, and margin notes
• Every quote lands in one searchable hub
• Built-in dictionary with offline definitions and lookup history

**PREMIUM (OPTIONAL)**
• Google Drive sync across devices
• Background read-aloud with screen locked
• Custom font upload, extra highlight colors, page thumbnails
• Ad-free reading

ReadNest is free for core reading with ads. Your files stay on your device —
we never upload your books.
